09 / Project notes

This build measures audio and offers cautious diagnostic guidance. It does not yet provide true multitrack source separation, perceptual masking proof, AI listening, or reference-master matching.

HSP Home Analyzer · Local-first browser tool · Not a substitute for critical listening on multiple playback systems.
